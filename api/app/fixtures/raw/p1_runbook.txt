P1 Incident Triage (High Severity)
- Confirm impact: customer checkout/payment affected, store operations blocked, or widespread outage.
- Declare P1 in the incident channel and assign roles: Incident Commander, Comms Lead, Tech Lead.
- First 10 minutes: stabilize. Roll back the latest change if suspected. Validate monitoring signals.
- Gather timeline, affected services, and current mitigation status.
- Escalate to on-call teams and vendor support if required.
