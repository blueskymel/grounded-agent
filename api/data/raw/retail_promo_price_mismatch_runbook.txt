Title: Retail Promo Price Mismatch Runbook

Trigger Conditions:
- Promotion discount not applied at POS in 3 or more stores
- Price mismatch complaints exceed 10 customers in 15 minutes

Initial Response:
1. Declare SEV3 incident and assign owner.
2. Capture sample SKUs, expected promo price, and actual charged price.
3. Freeze additional promo config publishes until root cause is known.

Diagnosis Checklist:
- Verify active promo window (start/end timezone).
- Verify store catalog replication status.
- Validate POS cache version against central pricing service.
- Check feature flag state for promo engine.

Mitigation Options:
- Force cache refresh on impacted stores.
- Roll back most recent pricing ruleset if mismatch started after deploy.
- Enable manual discount override guidance for cashiers.

Communication:
- Incident updates every 30 minutes in #incident-bridge.
- Notify customer support with affected SKU list and remediation script.

Recovery Criteria:
- Test basket in 3 impacted stores shows correct promo price.
- No new pricing mismatch alerts for 30 minutes.

Post-Incident:
- Document root cause category: data sync, flag config, or rules logic.
- Add automated pre-publish validation for promo rules.
