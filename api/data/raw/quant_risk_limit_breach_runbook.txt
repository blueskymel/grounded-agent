Title: Quant Risk Limit Breach Runbook

Objective:
Respond quickly when portfolio, strategy, or instrument risk limits are breached.

Breach Examples:
- Strategy gross exposure exceeds configured cap.
- Intraday drawdown exceeds stop threshold.
- Order notional exceeds symbol risk guardrail.

Immediate Actions:
1. Freeze new order placement for impacted strategy.
2. Notify risk officer and incident commander.
3. Capture breach snapshot: time, strategy, symbol, exposure, pnl.

Validation:
- Confirm breach is not due to delayed positions feed.
- Recompute limits using latest positions and marks.
- Verify risk config version and recent changes.

Containment:
- Force strategy kill switch if breach persists.
- Reduce open risk by flattening highest-risk legs first.
- Block manual override unless approved by risk officer.

Escalation:
- SEV1 if breach impacts firm-level limits.
- SEV2 for strategy-level breaches lasting > 10 minutes.

Exit Criteria:
- Exposure returns within approved limits.
- Risk and trading operations approve strategy resume.
- Incident timeline and actions logged in ticket.
